# UI/UX Guardrails


- Do not invent colors, gradients, shadows, spacing, icons, charts, copy tone, or navigation.
- Use approved tokens and reusable components first.
- No neon, heavy gradients, broad glassmorphism, decorative charts, horizontal scroll, nested tabs, emoji icons, or motivational finance copy.
- Max 1 primary CTA per section.
- Max 1 main graph per screen.
- Max 2 hierarchy levels.
- Dashboard max: 1 summary strip, 1 trend chart, 1 category visualization, 5 recent transactions, 1 AI insight.


## Dashboard Cap

- 1 summary strip
- 1 useful trend chart
- 1 category visualization
- 5 recent transactions
- 1 AI insight block

## Reports Cap

- 1 comparison chart
- 1 breakdown section
- 1 insight section
- 1 export CTA

## Empty State Template

- Lucide icon
- Short title
- One-line subtitle
- Optional single CTA
