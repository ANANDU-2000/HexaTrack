# Reports Wireframe

## Section Order
1. Header and period selector
2. One main comparison chart
3. One breakdown section
4. One insight section
5. One export CTA

## Sticky Areas
- No sticky filter wall.
- Export remains secondary.

## Spacing Hierarchy
- Charts sit in standard cards.
- Filters use progressive reveal.

## Scroll Rules
- Keep the primary job within two mobile screens.
- Move advanced details to secondary screens or progressive disclosure.

## Forbidden
- No 10 charts.
- No nested tabs.
- No analytics warehouse UI.
